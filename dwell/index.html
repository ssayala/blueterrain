100% Free. No subscriptions, no ads.

Be Present

No buttons, no timers. Dwell gracefully recognizes when you arrive and leave your favorite places, so you can stay focused on the moment.

See Your Rhythm

Visualize the flow of your week with elegant charts, 16-week activity heatmaps, and arrival/departure patterns that reveal how you truly spend your time.

Step Logging

See the steps you take at each location via Apple Health. Understand not just where you spend time, but how active you are while you’re there.

Driving Insights

Automatically see time spent on the road each day — no setup required. Your commute is part of your rhythm too.

Smart Reminders

Set time goals for your favorite places and get a gentle alert when you’ve hit them. Stay intentional about where your time goes.

Thoughtful Design

Mindful filtering ignores quick stops and errands. Dwell only captures the stays that truly matter to your story.

Privacy is a Right

Most apps make money by selling your movements. We believe your privacy is a right, not a luxury. Dwell is free, private, and always yours. Your data is stored locally and in your private iCloud. We never see it, and we never sell it.
