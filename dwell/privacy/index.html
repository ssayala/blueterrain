Dwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

Short version: Dwell does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your location history and saved places never leave your device.

No Data Collection

Dwell does not collect any personal information. Your saved places, geofences, and visit history are stored entirely on-device in a local SQLite database. Nothing is sent to external servers, and we have no visibility into your data.

Location Access

Dwell requests Always On location permission so it can detect when you arrive at and depart from your saved places, even when the app is in the background. Your location is processed entirely on your device using iOS geofencing (CoreLocation). Raw GPS coordinates are never stored — only the time you entered and exited each saved place.

What the App Stores Locally

  • Your saved places (name, address, geofence radius)
  • Visit records (place, entry time, exit time)
  • App settings and preferences

All of this data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes it entirely.

Notifications

Dwell can send local notifications to summarize your time at a place after you leave. These notifications are scheduled on-device by iOS and are never routed through external servers. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Dwell.

No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

Dwell contains no advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, crash reporters, or any other third-party code that could collect or transmit data.

No Account Required

Dwell does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Children's Privacy

Because Dwell collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
