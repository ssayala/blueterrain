Privacy Policy

AguaTrack

Last updated: February 28, 2026

Short version: AguaTrack does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

No Data Collection

AguaTrack does not collect any personal information. Your water intake logs, settings, and daily goals are stored entirely on your device using Apple's built-in storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). Nothing is sent to external servers, and we have no visibility into your data.

What the App Stores Locally

  • Your water intake entries (date, time, amount in ounces)
  • Daily goal and notification preferences
  • Quick-add preset amounts

All of this data lives only on your device. Deleting the app removes it entirely.

Notifications

AguaTrack can send local reminders to drink water. These notifications are scheduled on-device by iOS and are never routed through external servers. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → AguaTrack.

No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

AguaTrack contains no advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, crash reporters, or any other third-party code that could collect or transmit data.

No Account Required

AguaTrack does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

Children's Privacy

Because AguaTrack collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.
